Product page: Lumen K2 Desk Lamp

The Lumen K2 is an LED desk lamp from Brightwell Home, released on September 15, 2026. It offers five color temperatures and a built-in wireless phone charger in the base.

Price: $89.99. Ships free within the United States from Brightwell's warehouse in Columbus, Ohio.

The arm folds flat for storage, and the lamp draws 9 watts at full brightness. It comes with a two-year warranty.
